Keyword search only finds papers that use your words. Snowball starts from papers you already trust, follows how science actually connects, and returns new papers, ranked, each with a reason you can check. Every result is a real PubMed, DOI or Europe PMC record; the model ranks and explains, it never writes a citation.

Finds what your papers point to

Which papers belong next to the ones I already trust?

  • Citations: what your papers cite, and newer work that cites them.
  • Co-cited: papers cited alongside yours, weighted so famous papers don't win by fame.
  • PubMed similar: PubMed's own nearest neighbours of each paper.
  • Search and Web: Nemotron-written searches on PubMed, preprints and the web, for work too new to be cited yet.

Shows why each paper is there

Can I trust a result without opening it?

  • Nemotron 3.5 Lightning screens the strongest 150 against what you're looking for, and grades each 0–3.
  • Nemotron 3 Super explains the top 25: does it support, contradict, extend or review your papers?
  • Each quote is checked against the abstract. If it isn't there, it isn't shown.
  • Retracted papers never make the list; they are named in "Left out".

How it works

  1. 1Your papersPMIDs, DOIs, links, PMC or OpenAlex IDs, or titles, resolved to real records.
  2. 2Five channelsCitations, Co-cited, PubMed similar, Search and Web, all at once. Each hit remembers where it came from.
  3. 3CombineDuplicates, retracted papers and non-articles are left out, each with its reason.
  4. 4ScreenCitations, similarity and meaning pick the strongest 150; Lightning grades them against your rubric.
  5. 5ExplainSuper writes why the top 25 matter, naming your papers as S1, S2…
  6. 6You decideEvery score is four labelled parts you can check. Export to Zotero as RIS.
Runs on Nebius Token Factory (Nemotron 3 Super, Nemotron 3.5 Lightning, Qwen3-Embedding-8B) with NIH iCite, PubMed, Europe PMC and Tavily. The server needs NEBIUS_API_KEY; OPENALEX_API_KEY adds semantic search and NCBI_API_KEY makes PubMed faster. API reference at /docs.
