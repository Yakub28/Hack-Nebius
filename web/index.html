Can I trust a result without reading the whole paper?

  • An LLM grades every strong candidate 0–4 against criteria drawn from your question.
  • Your seeds are graded too, which draws a relevance line: above it, as relevant as your seeds.
  • Each result quotes its abstract, and the quote is checked word for word. Quotes that fail are dropped.
  • Every link to a seed is a citation or a similarity score you can check.

How it works

  1. 1ResolveSeeds given as PMIDs, DOIs, PubMed links or titles are found in PubMed.
  2. 2PlanThe LLM turns the question and seeds into inclusion criteria and search queries.
  3. 3CollectSeven channels gather candidates. Each one records where it came from.
  4. 4RankEmbedding similarity to each seed and to the question, fused with citation signals.
  5. 5JudgeUp to ~180 of the strongest candidates are graded against the criteria, with evidence.

Runs on Nebius Token Factory (Qwen3-Embedding-8B for retrieval, Qwen3-235B as the judge) and Tavily web search. The server needs NEBIUS_API_KEY; TAVILY_API_KEY switches on web search and NCBI_API_KEY makes PubMed faster. API reference at /docs.
